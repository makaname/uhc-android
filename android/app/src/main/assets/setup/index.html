Homestuck Collection

The app needs the Unofficial Homestuck Collection's asset pack (Asset Pack V2, about 4.4 GB). It stays on your phone; nothing is downloaded.

  1. 1.

    Copy UHC-Asset-Pack-V2.zip from your PC to your phone, for example into Download. Don't unzip it; the app reads it as is.

    LocalSend, a USB cable or anything else works.

  2. 2.

    Show the app where it is. You only do this once; the app can read that one file and nothing else on your phone.
