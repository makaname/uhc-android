Homestuck Collection

The app needs the Unofficial Homestuck Collection's asset pack (Asset Pack V2, about 4.4 GB). It stays on your phone; nothing is downloaded.

  1. 1.

    Copy UHC-Asset-Pack-V2.zip from your PC to your phone's Download folder. Don't unzip it; the app reads it as is.

    LocalSend, a USB cable or anything else works. An unpacked Asset Pack V2 folder works too.

  2. 2.

    Let the app read files on your phone.

  3. 3.

    Point the app at the folder.
